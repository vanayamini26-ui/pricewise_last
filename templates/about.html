About PriceWise

PriceWise is a B.Tech project that helps you decide where to buy an electronic product: Amazon India or Flipkart.

How to use it

  1. Search for a product, for example "Samsung Galaxy S24".
  2. PriceWise reads the current listed price on both stores.
  3. Compare the two prices and the difference, then open the store you prefer.

What to know

  • Prices are read live when you search. A repeat search within two minutes may reuse the earlier result, and you can use "Refresh prices" to read the stores again.
  • Only the same product variant is compared, so a 128 GB phone is never compared with a 256 GB one.
  • If a store cannot be reached, PriceWise says "Data unavailable" and never shows an old or estimated price.
  • Prices are the listed prices. Coupons and bank offers applied at checkout are not included.
  • Only electronics are shown. PriceWise is not connected to Amazon or Flipkart.
